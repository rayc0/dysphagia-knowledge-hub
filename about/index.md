# About softmeal.org

softmeal.org is a dysphagia knowledge hub from **SeniorDeli (Carewells)**, founded by **Raymond Chau Yik-Chun (周益俊)**. It brings together educational articles on texture-modified meals, swallowing standards and caregiving.

The information supports conversations with a person's care team. A clinician's individual assessment and care plan determine the food texture, drink consistency, positioning and assistance appropriate for that person. An article or a texture test does not replace that assessment.

Follow the primary references linked in each guide to check its source and scope. For information about SeniorDeli, visit [seniordeli.com](https://www.seniordeli.com/).
