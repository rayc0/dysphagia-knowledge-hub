# Choosing a Blender for Texture Modification


A blender can help prepare foods and drinks, but its type and the appearance of the result do not show that a prescribed texture has been met. Ask the clinician or care team which IDDSI level is intended and how the result should be checked.

## What IDDSI testing says

The [IDDSI Testing Methods (July 2019)](https://www.iddsi.org/images/Publications-Resources/DetailedDefnTestMethods/English/V2TestingMethodsEnglish31july2019.pdf) (p. 2) explains that tests describe a product at the time of testing and should use intended serving conditions, especially temperature. It also states that a clinician selects foods and drinks based on comprehensive assessment. A blender, recipe name, processing time, or appearance therefore does not prove a prescribed texture. Complete the full applicable IDDSI test rather than relying on a visual check.

## Compare workflow options

Countertop, handheld, and processor options can support different meal-preparation workflows; the category alone is not a texture-level guarantee. Compare them with the care team against the actual meal plan.

- **Countertop blender:** Ask the seller how the intended quantities compare with the manufacturer’s stated capacity and minimum fill. Where will the jug and other parts be cleaned?
- **Handheld blender:** Ask whether the care plan calls for blending in a serving vessel, and which parts the manufacturer lists for that use. Confirm cleaning instructions for each part.
- **Processor:** Ask whether a separate processing step is wanted, which ingredients and quantities are permitted by the manual, and how removable parts are cleaned.

## Questions before buying

Ask the seller for the manufacturer’s manual or written answers to these questions:

1. **Capacity and minimum fill:** What are the maximum and minimum amounts for each container or attachment intended for the planned meal preparation?
2. **Permitted ingredients:** Does the manual permit every ingredient and combination in the care plan?
3. **Liquid temperature:** Which liquid and ingredient temperatures are permitted?
4. **Cleaning and dishwasher use:** Which parts may be washed in a dishwasher, and what cleaning method does the manual require for the others?
5. **Duty cycle:** How often and for how long may the appliance run? Does the manual specify pauses, cooling, or operating limits?
6. **Warranty and local service:** What warranty applies, and where can warranty service or replacement parts be obtained locally?

## Check the result with the care team

Before using a blender, ask which full applicable IDDSI test to complete for each food or drink and under which intended serving conditions, especially temperature. Follow the manufacturer’s instructions and the prescribed recipe. Never add unprescribed thin liquid just to make blending easier; if the clinician has not specified it, ask the care team how to proceed.

If a test does not match the prescribed texture, ask the clinician or care team what to do rather than treating the blend as suitable.
